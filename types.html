Types of DDoS Attacks

Common Types

  • Volume-based attacks – overload bandwidth
  • Protocol attacks – exhaust server resources
  • Application-layer attacks – target web apps

How Websites Protect Themselves

  • Firewalls
  • Rate limiting
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)
  • DDoS protection services

DDoS attacks are illegal. Learn defense, not attack.
