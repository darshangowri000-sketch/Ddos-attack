What is a DDoS Attack?

Educational website for learning purposes only

Definition

A Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack is a cyberattack where many systems send a large amount of traffic to a server, causing it to slow down or become unavailable.

Why DDoS Attacks Are Dangerous

  • Websites go offline
  • Users cannot access services
  • Businesses lose money and trust
